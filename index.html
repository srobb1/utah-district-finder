Data Source

          
Utah UGRC / SGID

        

      


      

        All district data is queried live from the Utah Geospatial Resource Center's
        State Geographic Information Datasource (SGID). District boundaries reflect
        the 2026 court-ordered congressional redistricting and the 2022 post-census
        redistricting for state legislative districts. Precinct data is submitted by
        county clerks and may lag behind boundary changes.
