Elections & Voting

Morgan County residents can use the tool at right to look up their political districts by address. Enter your street address to instantly see your U.S. Congressional district, Utah State Senate and House districts, State School Board district, voting precinct, and more.

Voter registration is handled through the Utah Lieutenant Governor's office. Contact the Morgan County Clerk's office for questions about local elections, polling locations, or absentee ballots.

District boundaries reflect the 2026 court-ordered congressional redistricting and the 2022 post-census redistricting for state legislative districts. Data is pulled live from the Utah UGRC State Geographic Information Datasource.
