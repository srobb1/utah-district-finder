Step 1 — Prepare your CSV

The app auto-detects your column format. Any of these work:

Format A — Single address column
address e.g. "3572 N Morgan Valley Dr, Morgan, UT 84050"
Format B — Street + Zone columns
street, city or street, zip e.g. "3572 N Morgan Valley Dr", "84050"
Format C — Split street columns
street_number, street_name, city, zip e.g. "3572", "N Morgan Valley Dr", "Morgan", "84050"
